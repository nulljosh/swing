How it works

You get matched

One tap, one person. No profile, no sign-up, no waiting room.

The clock runs

Fifteen seconds. Both of you have to tap for more time, or the call ends by itself and you each get somebody new.

You keep them, or you don't

If you both tap Keep, you swap handles. If only one of you does, nothing is shared and neither of you is told.

The plain version

  • Video never touches our server. Calls are peer-to-peer. Nothing is recorded and nothing is stored.
  • You are anonymous until you both choose otherwise. There are no accounts.
  • 18 or older. Nudity, harassment, and minors are not allowed, and Report cuts the call.
  • It is early. Reports are logged, not policed by anyone yet, and peers behind strict firewalls may fail to connect.
